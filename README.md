# runneth-studio

A content studio for your videos, built for Runneth. Every video you work on becomes one project with one folder and one card in your studio. Open the card to pick your takes, mark your b-roll and review each edit, all in one place.

Made by [Alysha](https://github.com/alyshadotmd) while building her own AI video editing stack.

## The skill

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| [`edit-studio`](skills/edit-studio/SKILL.md) | Names each video, makes its project folder, and gives it one card in your content studio with Takes, B-roll and Review edit tabs. Every editing step adds to that same project. |

## What you get

- **One project per video.** A name like "Buoy founder ad breakdown", a folder with `raw/a-roll`, `raw/b-roll`, `b-roll-picks`, `drafts`, `final`, `script.md` and `version-log.md`, and one card in the studio.
- **🎬 Takes:** every take of every line, side by side. Heart the best one, play your cut, copy your picks back.
- **🎥 B-roll:** scrub each b-roll video, mark the moments you want and say why, or see the clips that were kept.
- **🎞️ Review edit:** every version of the edit. Pause, leave a note at that moment, copy your notes back, approve the one you like.

## How to use it

Add the `edit-studio` folder to your skills in Runneth. The first time you work on a video, it builds your studio. Runneth only for now.

Pairs with the editing skills in [video-editing](https://github.com/alyshadotmd/video-editing).
